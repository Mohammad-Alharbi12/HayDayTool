Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

HayDay Tool (“the app,” “we,” “us”) is a free, fan-made web tool for the game Hay Day. This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to information when you use it. The short version: we don’t have accounts, we don’t collect your personal information, and the lists you build stay on your own device.

Information we collect

None that identifies you. There is no sign-up, no login, and no account. We do not ask for your name, email, or any personal details, and we have no server that stores anything about you.

Data you create in the app

Your Needed Goods list, your barn and silo sizes, and your other preferences are saved only in your own browser using its local storage. This data never leaves your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. If you clear your browser data, use private/incognito mode, or switch to a different device or browser, that saved data won’t carry over.

Cookies and tracking

We do not use cookies, analytics, advertising, or any third-party tracking. We don’t build a profile of you or follow you around the web.

Third-party services

To show live game data, the app requests publicly available Hay Day information from hayday.info, sometimes through public proxy services that make that data accessible in the browser. As with any normal web request, those services may receive standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type. We don’t control those services and aren’t responsible for their practices; please refer to their own policies.

The app is hosted on GitHub Pages (a service of GitHub, Inc.). GitHub may automatically log standard request information, such as IP addresses, in its server logs. See GitHub’s privacy statement for details.

Children’s privacy

The app is a general-audience tool and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we’ll change the “Effective” date above. Continued use of the app means you accept the current version.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email support@haydaytool.com or reach out through the project’s GitHub page.
